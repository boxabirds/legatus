# Decision log

Status: draft, 2026-10-07. Owner of this file: writer W-A.

This file lists every decision of the proxy specification. Later files cite a decision by its ID. A new decision gets the next free number. A decision never changes its number.

## How to read this log

Status has three values:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| decided by owner | The owner stated the decision in a recorded text. The evidence column links the text. |
| proposed by coordinator | The coordinator chose the decision or the value. The owner did not approve it yet. A value marked PROPOSED is a default. |
| open | Nobody decided. The decision needs a measurement or an owner answer. |

A proposed row can carry one of two notes. The note "pending owner confirmation" means that the text uses the default until the owner answers. The note "owner decision pending" means that the owner must choose before the text is final.

The status "decided by owner" can carry the note "value PROPOSED". The owner decided the rule, and the number stays PROPOSED until a measurement.

On 2026-10-07 the owner approved the open items of the first draft. These rows carry the date 2026-10-07 in the reason column.

Dates are 2026-10-06 or 2026-10-07. The scope reset and the first cross-check happened on 2026-10-06. The proxy brief and the research happened on 2026-10-07.

A limit of this log: the brief for this specification states the decisions but not who made each one. This log marks a decision as "decided by owner" only when a recorded owner text supports it. Where no such text exists, the status is "proposed by coordinator". The owner must approve or change these rows.
